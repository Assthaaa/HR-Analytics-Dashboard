HR Analytics Dashboard
1. Project Title / Headline
    HR Workforce Insights: Global Employee Analytics Dashboard

A dynamic, interactive data visualization tool built to track workforce demographics, employee attrition patterns, salary distributions, and department-wise performance metrics.

2. Short Description / Purpose
The HR Analytics Dashboard is a visually engaging and analytical Power BI report designed to help users explore and analyze key workforce metrics across 580 total employees and multiple departments. The dashboard focuses on highlighting critical HR insights like active employee counts, attrition rates, departmental turnover, salary slabs, and experience trends. This tool is intended for use by HR analysts, people managers, and organizational strategists who seek to understand attrition drivers and improve employee retention globally.

3. Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop - Main data visualization platform used for report creation.

🔄 Power Query - Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.

⚙️ DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) - Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.

🔗 Data Modeling - Relationships established among tables to enable cross-filtering and seamless navigation.

4. Key Metrics & KPIs Tracked
Total Employees: 580

Active Employees: 471

Attrition Count: 109 employees

Attrition Rate: 19% overall turnover rate

Average Age: ~31 years old

5. Key Visualizations & Features
Attrition by Department (Donut Chart): Visualizes the percentage distribution of turnover across departments like Sales (55%), Administration, and others.

Department-Wise Employee Count (Bar Chart): Compares total workforce size across various business units.

Attrition by Salary Slab (Bar Chart): Analyzes turnover trends across different compensation tiers (e.g., 0-3 LPA, 3-6 LPA, 6-10 LPA, 10+ LPA).

Job Role & Satisfaction Matrix (Table): Cross-tabulates specific job roles against employee job satisfaction ratings (Levels 1 to 4).

Attrition by Gender (Donut Chart): Compares employee turnover rates between male and female demographics.

Attrition Trend by Experience (Line Chart): Maps resignation rates against total years of professional experience.

6. Business Impact & Insights
Targeted Retention: HR leaders can pinpoint high-turnover departments like Sales and specific compensation brackets to optimize retention efforts.

Satisfaction Correlation: Management can evaluate how low job satisfaction ratings map directly to departures across specific job titles.

Experience Mapping: Helps identify critical career milestones where employees are most likely to leave, aiding in better mid-career engagement.

Executive Decision Support: Replaces static reporting with interactive slicers (such as age group filters ranging from 18 to 55) for rapid, data-driven planning.
